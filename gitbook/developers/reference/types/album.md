# Album

The messages created by one album request, in album order.

| Field | Type | Required | Description and constraints |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `messages` | Array of [Message](message.md) | Yes | These are the messages returned by the operation, in result order. The minimum is 2 items. The maximum is 10 items. |
