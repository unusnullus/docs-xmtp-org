# 15.4 Risk warning

Buying, holding, or using ARX tokens involves a high level of risk, including the potential loss of value, technical failures, or total loss of funds.

Participants should only engage with ARX if they fully understand the nature of crypto-assets and the associated financial, technological, and regulatory risks.\
ARX tokens are not backed by any government, financial institution, or guarantee fund.

Users are responsible for safeguarding their private keys and ensuring they comply with all applicable laws in their jurisdiction.
