# 1.4 Rise of Web3 Identity

Web3 infrastructure introduces a paradigm where ownership and identity are verifiable without being centralized. By combining **decentralized networks**, **self-custodial wallets**, and **programmable smart contracts**, Web3 enables individuals to manage their digital presence directly.

### The Architectural Shift
In this model, the traditional dependency on centralized providers is replaced by cryptographic proofs.

| Feature | Web2 Identity | Web3 Identity (ARX) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Control** | Centralized (Google, Meta, etc.) | Self-Sovereign (User-controlled) |
| **Authentication** | Passwords / Personal identifiers | Cryptographic private keys |
| **Data Storage** | Corporate silos | Decentralized ledgers / IPFS |
| **Verification** | Third-party validation | On-chain verification |

### Bridging Anonymity and Accountability
This framework solves a long-standing digital dilemma: how to ensure a user follows system rules without requiring them to surrender their real-world identity.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Pseudonymous Verifiability**
Users authenticate with cryptographic credentials. Transactions and communications are verified on-chain, proving the *validity* of an action without exposing the *private info* of the actor.
{% endhint %}
