# 8 Economic Model

The ARX economic model is built to maintain long-term sustainability without relying on advertising, custodial profits, or speculative token appreciation.&#x20;

It functions as a closed, circular economy where participants are rewarded based on their contribution to privacy, uptime, and decentralization. Over time, ARX transitions from inflation-driven incentives to a self-sustaining model powered by real user demand for communication, payment, and connectivity services.

This section covers:

* Circular economy design and value distribution
* Revenue sources and fee structure
* Proof-of-Relay reward allocation
* Deflationary and governance mechanisms ensuring long-term balance
