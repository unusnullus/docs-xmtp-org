# 9.1 DAO structure

The ARX DAO serves as the central coordination layer of the ecosystem, managing network evolution, treasury assets, and governance procedures through smart contracts.

**Governance process**:

* **Proposal creation**: Any ARX holder who meets the staking threshold can create a proposal on topics such as upgrades, funding, or parameter changes.
* **Discussion period**: Each proposal is reviewed in the governance forum, allowing community input and revisions.
* **Voting phase**: Token holders vote directly or delegate their voting rights to trusted representatives.
* **Execution**: Approved proposals are executed automatically through on-chain contracts, ensuring transparency and eliminating intermediary control.

This structure keeps decision-making efficient while guaranteeing community participation and accountability.
