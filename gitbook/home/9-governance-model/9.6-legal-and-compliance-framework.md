# 9.6 Legal and compliance framework

The ARX DAO operates under a Swiss association model that provides legal recognition while preserving decentralization. The association acts as the DAO’s legal representative in regulatory, contractual, and institutional contexts.

**Key functions**:

* **Legal personality**: Enables the DAO to hold assets, enter agreements, and operate transparently under Swiss jurisdiction.
* **Regulatory alignment**: Ensures compliance with MiCA and GDPR requirements through periodic legal reviews.
* **Stability and recognition**: Switzerland’s blockchain legislation provides clear guidance for DAOs, supporting global cooperation with institutions and regulators.

This model gives ARX the legal clarity required to operate internationally while maintaining its decentralized structure and governance autonomy.
