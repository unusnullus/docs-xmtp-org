# 13.8 Summary

ARX’s legal and compliance framework ensures that the project operates responsibly within the evolving global regulatory landscape.\
By combining Swiss jurisdictional strength, MiCA readiness, GDPR compliance, and a tiered KYC model, ARX delivers **compliance by design** without compromising decentralization or privacy.

This framework positions ARX as a credible and future-proof platform capable of engaging institutions, regulators, and users with full transparency and confidence.
