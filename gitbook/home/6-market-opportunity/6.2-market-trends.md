# 6.2 Market trends

* **Messenger monetization fatigue**: Users are leaving ad-driven and data-harvesting platforms in favor of private ecosystems that align with user interests.
* **Utility consolidation within chat apps**: WeChat and Telegram have demonstrated that messaging platforms can evolve into multifunctional ecosystems. ARX takes this further by combining encrypted messaging with self-custodial finance and decentralized connectivity.
* **Web3-native coordination**: The rise of wallet-based identity and smart contract automation makes messaging the most intuitive layer for decentralized coordination.
* **Infrastructure decentralization**: Validator-based relays and Proof-of-Relay consensus enhance uptime and resistance to censorship compared to traditional server architectures.
* **Regulatory readiness**: Frameworks like MiCA and GDPR are shaping compliant digital ecosystems. ARX’s Swiss structure and tiered KYC model align privacy with regulatory transparency.
