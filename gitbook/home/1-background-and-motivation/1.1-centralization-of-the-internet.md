# 1.1 Centralization of the internet

Over the past two decades, the internet has evolved from an open, community-driven network into an environment dominated by centralized platforms. These corporations control how people communicate, share data, and transact. While this growth connected billions, it created a structural imbalance: user data and privacy became the price of participation.

Centralization has led to loss of autonomy, censorship, and dependency on intermediaries.\
ARX is built to reverse this trend by decentralizing communication, identity, and financial tools so users regain ownership of their digital presence.
