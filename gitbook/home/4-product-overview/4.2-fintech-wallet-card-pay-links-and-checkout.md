# 4.2 Fintech – Wallet, Card, Pay-Links, and Checkout

The ARX Fintech Suite connects communication with financial sovereignty. It merges a multi-chain wallet, crypto card, and checkout system into a single, self-custodial environment that allows users to send, receive, and spend digital assets seamlessly.

### ARX Wallet

* **Multi-chain support**: Works across Ethereum, EVM networks, and Layer 2 solutions.
* **In-chat transactions**: Send or request tokens directly inside conversations.
* **Stablecoin integration**: Enables everyday payments with minimal volatility.
* **Swaps and conversions**: Built-in decentralized exchange for token swaps.
* **Gas abstraction**: Supports meta-transactions and fee delegation for smooth user experience.
* **Local key custody**: Private keys are generated and stored on the device, never on a centralized server.

### ARX Crypto card

* **Fiat bridge**: Instant crypto-to-fiat conversion through regulated partners.
* **Global acceptance**: Works on major card networks for everyday spending.
* **Instant settlement**: Transactions are processed directly from the wallet balance, eliminating intermediaries.
* **Tiered verification**: Light KYC for basic users, enhanced verification for higher transaction limits.
* **Premium cards**: Physical options include metal, gold, and silver-backed editions.

### Pay-links and checkout

* **Smart pay-links**: Create one-click payment links that can split or escrow funds between multiple parties.
* **QR payments**: Generate QR codes for in-person or event-based transactions.
* **Merchant SDK and API**: Integrate ARX checkout into websites, e-commerce platforms, or marketplaces.
* **Multi-asset support**: Accepts any supported token or stablecoin.
* **Instant settlement**: Payments are made directly to merchant wallets without custodians or delays.

The Fintech vertical transforms ARX into a full-stack commerce and payment ecosystem. It enables peer-to-peer, business-to-consumer, and international transactions that operate entirely within the user’s control. The combination of wallet, card, and payment tools ensures both liquidity and usability without compromising decentralization or privacy.
