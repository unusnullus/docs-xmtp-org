# 14.8 Summary

ARX acknowledges that participation in decentralized ecosystems involves inherent risks.\
Through proactive compliance, continuous audits, transparent governance, and strong user education, ARX aims to mitigate these risks wherever possible.

Nonetheless, users, partners, and investors must recognize that token value, network performance, and adoption cannot be guaranteed.\
By disclosing these risks openly, ARX ensures alignment with MiCA’s consumer protection requirements and reinforces its commitment to transparency, integrity, and responsible innovation.
