# 10 Roadmap

The ARX roadmap outlines the project’s evolution from prototype to a fully decentralized ecosystem. It follows a clear, deliverable-based timeline covering product launches, token deployment, and infrastructure expansion.  Each milestone introduces a new functional layer, including communication, payments, connectivity, and governance, building toward a unified, privacy-first network owned by its users.

The roadmap reflects ARX’s commitment to technical execution, progressive decentralization and regulatory readiness.
