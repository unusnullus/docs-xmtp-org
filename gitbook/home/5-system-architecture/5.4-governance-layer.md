# 5.4 Governance layer

Governance defines how ARX evolves over time through transparent, community-led processes. All proposals, votes, and fund allocations occur on-chain.

Key functions:

* **Proposal system**: Token holders can suggest upgrades, funding initiatives, or parameter changes.
* **Voting**: Token-weighted voting ensures representation and allows delegation of votes to trusted parties.
* **Treasury management**: The DAO treasury funds ecosystem development and operations, with all spending recorded on-chain.
* **Transitional safety**: A multisignature system ensures oversight during the transition to full decentralization.

This layer guarantees transparency and accountability for every strategic and technical decision within the network.
