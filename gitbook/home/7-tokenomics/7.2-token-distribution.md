# 7.2 Token distribution

| **Category**             | **Allocation** | **Vesting**                      | **Notes**                                                            |
| ------------------------ | -------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ecosystem and growth** | 25%            | Dynamic                          | Incentives, marketing campaigns, and strategic expansion             |
| **Treasury**             | 20%            | DAO controlled                   | Long-term sustainability and operational funding                     |
| **Investors**            | 15%            | 6-month cliff, 24-month vesting  | Early contributors, institutional supporters, and strategic partners |
| **Team**                 | 12%            | 12-month cliff, 36-month vesting | Founders and core contributors with long-term alignment              |
| **Liquidity**            | 10%            | Flexible                         | Exchange listings, market-making, and liquidity support              |
| **Partnerships**         | 8%             | Rolling                          | Strategic integrations and co-development initiatives                |
| **Community**            | 5%             | N/A                              | Airdrops, referral programs, and grassroots incentives               |
| **Node Rewards**         | 5%             | Bootstrap phase                  | Initial validator incentives for network launch                      |

This structure prioritizes stability, fairness, and sustainable ecosystem growth. The allocation to the DAO treasury and ecosystem fund ensures that ARX remains operationally independent and self-financed beyond its early stages.
