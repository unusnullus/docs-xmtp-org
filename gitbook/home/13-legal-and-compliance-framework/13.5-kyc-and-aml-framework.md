# 13.5 KYC and AML Framework

ARX adopts a **tiered identity model** that balances regulatory requirements with privacy.

| **Tier**                              | **Verification Level**                             | **Access**                                                       | **Description**                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tier 0 – Privacy Mode**             | No personal data, wallet-based authentication only | Basic messaging, small-value transactions                        | Enables pseudonymous participation while limiting exposure and transaction volume.  |
| **Tier 1 – Verified User**            | Light KYC (basic identity verification)            | Full app functionality, including VPN/eSIM and merchant payments | Designed for compliant access to regulated services while maintaining user privacy. |
| **Tier 2 – Business / Institutional** | Full KYC and AML screening                         | Merchant tools, API integrations, and higher transaction limits  | Ensures compliance for enterprise users and payment service partners.               |

KYC and AML processes are conducted through regulated third-party providers under Swiss and EU standards.\
Data is encrypted, stored securely, and used solely for verification purposes.
