# 9 Governance model

The ARX governance model defines how the network evolves, allocates resources, and maintains accountability through on-chain, community-driven decision-making. This section covers:

* DAO structure and operational flow.
* Treasury management and fund allocation principles.
* Governance roles and participation framework.
* Governance parameters and voting mechanisms.
* Decentralization roadmap and transition stages.
* Legal and compliance framework supporting the DAO.

Governance ensures that the ARX ecosystem operates transparently, sustainably, and without any centralized control. Every stakeholder who contributes to the network can participate in shaping its future through verified, on-chain procedures.
