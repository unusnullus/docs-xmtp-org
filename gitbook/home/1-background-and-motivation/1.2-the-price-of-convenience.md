# 1.2 The Price of Convenience

Most Web2 services operate on a model of **data extraction**. While these platforms appear "free," the hidden cost is the erosion of personal privacy and the loss of digital agency.

### The Hidden Cost of "Free"
Every interaction on traditional platforms generates a trail of metadata that is:
* **Stored** in centralized silos.
* **Analyzed** to build behavioral profiles.
* **Monetized** by third-party advertisers and data brokers.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**The Dependency Model**
Users do not own their accounts; they rent them. This corporate custody leads to:
1.  **Data Breaches:** Single points of failure exposing millions of private records.
2.  **Surveillance Misuse:** Unauthorized tracking and data harvesting.
3.  **Restricted Expression:** Arbitrary de-platforming and censorship.
    {% endhint %}
