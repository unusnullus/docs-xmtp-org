# 14.4 Key Management and Loss of Access

ARX is a **non-custodial ecosystem**, meaning users control their own cryptographic keys. Loss of access to these keys may result in permanent loss of funds or accounts.

### Risks include

* Misplacing seed phrases or recovery credentials.
* Device failure or unauthorized access.
* Lack of secure key backups.

### Mitigation

ARX provides educational resources and optional recovery mechanisms such as social recovery or encrypted cloud backups. However, ultimate responsibility for key management lies with the user.
