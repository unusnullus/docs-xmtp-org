# 12 Partnerships and Ecosystem

Partnerships form a strategic pillar of ARX’s development. They ensure reliability, compliance, and reach across communication, fintech, and connectivity verticals.\
ARX focuses on partnerships that directly strengthen its product layers:

* **Infrastructure integration**: Hosting providers support network performance and uptime today. Independent node operators are part of the planned network model.
* **Financial interoperability**: Collaboration with card issuers, payment networks, and stablecoin providers bridges crypto and fiat economies.
* **Connectivity expansion**: Telecom and eSIM partners extend ARX’s network to ensure global access under privacy-first conditions.

These collaborations transform ARX into a functional, scalable, and user-ready ecosystem.
