# 8.2 Revenue Streams

ARX generates diversified income from both blockchain and service layers.  The following structure ensures predictable and recurring revenues.

### Pro subscriptions

Premium app features such as enhanced storage, multi-device access, or message automation are available for monthly or annual subscriptions.

* Monthly price range: 9.99-19.99-29.99 USD (or equivalent in ARX).
* Estimated gross margin: 80%.
* 60% of subscription revenue goes to the DAO Treasury; 40% supports validator rewards and development.

### VPN, eSIM services and cloud storage

Each connection or storage session generates revenue based on bandwidth, data usage, or storage capacity.

* VPN rate: 4.99–9.99 USD per month
* eSIM rate: Varies based on regional data pack
* Cloud storage rate: 0.010–0.020 USD per GB per month (comparable to decentralized storage averages). Premium encrypted tiers may reach 0.025 USD/GB with enhanced redundancy and retrieval speed.
* Estimated user tiers:
  * Personal storage (up to 100 GB): 0.99–1.99 USD/month
  * Professional storage (500 GB–2 TB): 5.99–9.99 USD/month
  * Enterprise plans (>5 TB): custom, based on usage and replication.
* Revenue distribution:
  * 40% to storage node operators (for space and uptime)
  * 30% to the DAO Treasury
  * 20% to relay/VPN operators for bandwidth support
  * 10% to network maintenance and buyback reserves

This model introduces a steady, real-world revenue stream tied to user data demand. Each encrypted file stored contributes to validator income and DAO sustainability, reinforcing ARX’s decentralized infrastructure economy.

### Merchant and payment fees

Merchants using ARX PayLinks, checkout APIs, or SDKs pay small processing fees.

* Fee per transaction: 0.3 - 0.5%.
* Fee distribution: 50% to validators, 30% to the DAO, 20% to network maintenance.\
  This turns every in-app payment into a contribution to network security and sustainability.

### Transaction micro-fees

All on-chain transactions include a minimal cost to prevent spam and maintain validator uptime.

* Fee: approximately 0.001–0.002 ARX per transaction (depending on currency and chain)
* 50% proceeds go into the validator reward pool, and  50% goes to the treasury.

### DAO Treasury returns

The DAO invests part of its holdings in staking, liquidity pools, or strategic partnerships.\
Profits are used for buybacks, grants, and long-term stabilization. Together, these channels create a balanced economic mix: predictable subscription income, utility-driven service revenue, and cyclical DAO yields.
