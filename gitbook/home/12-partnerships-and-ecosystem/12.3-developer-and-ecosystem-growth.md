# 12.3 Developer and ecosystem growth

ARX provides open SDKs and APIs that allow third-party developers to build mini-apps, bots, and services within the ecosystem.

* **ARX SD**K: Messaging, wallet, and payment integration for developers.
* **Mini-apps framework**: Enables bots and lightweight utilities within ARX Messenger.
* **Ecosystem grants**: DAO-funded initiatives supporting infrastructure tools and regional adoption.

This approach ensures that ARX evolves beyond a static platform into a developer-driven ecosystem of integrated Web3 applications.
