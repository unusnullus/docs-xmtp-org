# 1.2 The price of convenience

Most Web2 services operate on data extraction. Every message, click, and connection generates metadata that is stored, analyzed, and monetized.\
Users pay for “free” services with their privacy, while their digital identities remain under corporate custody.

This dependency model has caused recurring data breaches, surveillance misuse, and restrictions on free expression.

ARX rejects this trade-off by giving users the same convenience of modern platforms without compromising control or privacy.
