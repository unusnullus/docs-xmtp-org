# 7.4 Economic flow and treasury sustainability

The ARX economy maintains a self-balancing structure where network activity continuously fuels ecosystem growth.

**Sources (inflows)**:

* Validator and relay rewards
* Staking yields
* Treasury disbursements
* Community and development grants

**Sinks (outflows)**:

* Transaction and subscription fees
* Merchant service fees for pay-links
* DAO-approved buybacks and burns

The ARX Treasury, managed by the DAO, acts as the central funding pool for network expansion and stability. It receives revenue from transaction fees, validator penalties, and integrations with partners.

Treasury funds are allocated to:

* Ecosystem development and grants
* Marketing and user acquisition
* Security audits and infrastructure maintenance
* Liquidity support and stabilization measures

This closed-loop design ensures the network remains financially autonomous and self-sustaining. As adoption grows, more economic activity directly reinforces validator rewards, liquidity, and long-term treasury health.
