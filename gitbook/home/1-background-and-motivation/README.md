# 1 Background & Motivation

The internet has entered a critical transition period.\
Centralized systems dominate how people communicate, transact, and connect - but at the cost of privacy, ownership, and autonomy.

ARX is built in response to this imbalance.\
It combines cryptographic identity, decentralized communication, and blockchain-based incentives to create a network where users own their data, assets and connectivity.

This section outlines the context behind ARX:

* how centralization reshaped the internet,
* why privacy and compliance are converging,
* and how Web3 technologies enable users to reclaim digital sovereignty.
