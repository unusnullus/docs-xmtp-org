# 12.2 Affiliate and Referral Growth Program

Partnerships extend beyond corporate alliances to include individuals and communities that help expand the ARX ecosystem. The Affiliate and Referral Growth Program forms part of ARX’s collaborative growth model, rewarding both strategic partners and individual advocates who contribute to ecosystem expansion.

### Program Objectives

* **Transparent contribution tracking**: Each referral or partnership activity is monitored through on-chain or dashboard-based identifiers, ensuring traceability and eliminating manipulation.
* **Community-driven growth**: Rather than relying on centralized marketing agencies, ARX channels resources directly to its community, rewarding organic outreach and real participation.
* **Partnership scalability**: The system supports referral links for individuals, influencer programs, venture partners, and node operators, creating a single unified framework for ecosystem incentives.

### Technical Framework

* **On-chain tracking**: Every referral event is registered through a smart contract or integrated dashboard to maintain accountability and auditability.
* **Tiered structure**: Different reward tiers apply depending on the participant’s role — individual referrer, validator, ecosystem partner, or institutional affiliate.
* **Reward distribution**: Rewards are paid in ARX tokens from a dedicated allocation within the community growth pool. Token issuance for referrals occurs only after verification and KYC clearance, ensuring compliance.

### Use Cases

1. **Token Sale Phase**:  Early participants receive unique referral IDs. Verified referrals earn a reward equivalent to 3-5% of the referred participant’s contribution, credited in ARX after the token generation event. This system ensures controlled, transparent growth during the sale without inflating overall supply.
2. **Ecosystem Expansion Phase**: After launch, the referral logic evolves into an affiliate framework used to onboard new merchants, developers, or node operators. Referrers can earn additional incentives when their referred partners begin contributing to network volume, governance participation, or infrastructure provisioning.
3. **Strategic Partnerships**: Institutional partners, accelerators, and venture ecosystems can integrate referral tracking APIs into their platforms, allowing joint user acquisition campaigns or B2B co-marketing initiatives.

### **Compliance and Transparency**

All referral rewards are disclosed publicly through DAO reporting. Smart contracts or verifiable dashboards ensure that referral-based token flows remain transparent and within the allocation defined in the tokenomics plan.\
The program complies with MiCA and FINMA requirements by operating as a marketing incentive, not as an investment or yield product.
