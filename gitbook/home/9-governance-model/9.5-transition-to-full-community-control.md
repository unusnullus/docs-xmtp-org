# 9.5 Transition to full community control

ARX follows a structured decentralization path that transitions from foundation-led oversight to full DAO governance.

### Phase 1: Foundation governance

The ARX Foundation manages treasury operations and network security through a controlled multi-signature structure.

### Phase 2: Hybrid DAO activation

The DAO becomes active with proposal submission, voting, and budget allocation. The Foundation shifts to a compliance and coordination role.

### Phase 3: Full DAO governance

Complete decentralization is achieved as the DAO assumes control of all governance, treasury, and upgrade processes. The Foundation remains only for legal and regulatory representation under Swiss law.This gradual transition ensures operational stability while empowering community ownership.
