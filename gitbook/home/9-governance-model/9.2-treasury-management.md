# 9.2 Treasury management

The ARX Treasury functions as the financial foundation of the network. It collects revenues, staking penalties, and unallocated tokens, distributing funds based on DAO-approved proposals.

### Primary funding areas

* Ecosystem growth: Grants for developers, validators, and research initiatives that expand ARX infrastructure.
* Operations: Financing for relay and validator nodes, audits, and infrastructure maintenance.
* Marketing and expansion: Funding user acquisition, partnerships, and educational outreach.
* Liquidity and stability: DAO-managed liquidity pools, listings, and buybacks to maintain token accessibility and market balance.

### Control and oversight

All treasury movements are publicly recorded on-chain. Expenditures require DAO approval, with multi-signature safety mechanisms during early stages. This guarantees responsible fund management and long-term sustainability.
