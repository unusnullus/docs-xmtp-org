# 13.1 Jurisdiction and Legal Entity

ARX operates through a Swiss-registered entity serving as the issuer and foundation responsible for coordination, token issuance, and early-stage governance. Switzerland was selected for its mature regulatory environment, blockchain-friendly legislation, and global reputation for legal clarity.

### Legal entity functions

* Token issuance and governance alignment.
* Coordination with Swiss and EU regulators.
* Custody of intellectual property and trademarks.
* Interface between the DAO and external legal or financial institutions.
* Compliance reporting and audit management.

The Swiss jurisdiction provides a stable and internationally recognized foundation for both crypto and financial innovation.
