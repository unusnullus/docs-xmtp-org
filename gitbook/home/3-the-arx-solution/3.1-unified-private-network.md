# 3.1 Unified private network

At the core of ARX is the unified private network, which merges communication, payments, and connectivity within a single identity layer. Each user interacts with the ecosystem through a cryptographic wallet that functions as both authentication and payment mechanism.

### Communication

The ARX messenger provides end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) communication for direct messages, group chats, and media sharing. Routing is handled by distributed relays that obscure sender and receiver metadata. No message history or interaction data is stored centrally, ensuring confidentiality and resilience.

### Payments

A built-in multi-chain wallet enables fast, peer-to-peer transfers, swaps, and merchant payments. Balances are fully self-custodial and accessible directly from the user’s device. Payment requests, QR-based transactions, and integrations with decentralized exchanges expand usability without compromising privacy.

### Connectivity

ARX extends privacy beyond communication into network access. Its decentralized VPN layer allows users to connect to the internet securely through validator nodes that expose no-log endpoints. The integrated eSIM solution, powered by 1GLOBAL infrastructure, enables private mobile connectivity across multiple regions.

All three layers, including communication, finance, and network, operate under a single wallet-based identity. No phone numbers, emails, or external accounts are required. Users retain control of their data while moving seamlessly across all ARX services.
