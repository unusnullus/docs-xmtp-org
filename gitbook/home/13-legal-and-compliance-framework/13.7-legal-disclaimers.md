# 13.7 Legal Disclaimers

To ensure regulatory clarity and consumer transparency, ARX includes the following disclaimers across its documentation and user interface:

* **No Financial Advice**: ARX does not provide investment or financial advisory services.
* **No Guaranteed Returns**: Token rewards are based on network participation and performance metrics, not fixed interest or yields.
* **Token Utility Statement**: The ARX token is designed exclusively for use within the network and should not be purchased for speculative purposes.
* **Jurisdiction Limitation**: Distribution and participation may be restricted in certain regions according to local regulations.
* **Forward-Looking Statements**: Roadmaps, forecasts, or financial expectations are not binding and may change based on technical or regulatory developments.

These disclosures protect both the issuer and users, maintaining transparency and adherence to global compliance standards.
