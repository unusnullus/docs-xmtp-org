# 1.4 Rise of Web3 identity

Web3 infrastructure introduces a paradigm where ownership and identity are verifiable but not centralized.\
By combining decentralized networks, self-custodial wallets, and programmable smart contracts, Web3 makes it possible for individuals to control their data, identity, and assets directly - without intermediaries.

In this model, users authenticate with cryptographic credentials instead of personal identifiers. Transactions, communications, and governance actions can be verified on-chain without exposing private information.

This framework bridges the long-standing gap between anonymity and accountability.  Users can remain pseudonymous yet still participate in verifiable, rule-based systems.

The evolution of Web3 identity provides the technical foundation for a new kind of internet - one where privacy is not negotiated through terms of service but embedded into the design itself.

This shift marks the transition from regulatory dependence to technological sovereignty, setting the stage for platforms built around self-custody, encryption, and community governance.
