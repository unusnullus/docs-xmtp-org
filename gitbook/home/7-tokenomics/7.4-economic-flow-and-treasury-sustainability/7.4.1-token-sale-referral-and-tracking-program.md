# 7.4.1 Token Sale Referral and Tracking Program

To support transparent and community-driven token distribution, ARX implements an internal referral system during the public sale phase. The program allows verified participants to generate unique referral links and track contributions in real time through the ARX dashboard.

Each verified referral is recorded on-chain, ensuring fairness and preventing abuse through multi-accounting or fraudulent activity. Referral rewards are distributed in ARX tokens at the close of the sale, based on the referred participant’s contribution tier. This system creates an organic incentive for community members, early supporters, and partners to promote ARX without relying on paid advertising.

The referral program operates exclusively during the token sale phase and has no impact on post-launch token emissions or circulating supply.
