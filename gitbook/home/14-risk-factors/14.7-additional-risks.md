# 14.7 Additional Risks

Beyond the above categories, users should consider broader risks inherent to decentralized networks, including:

* **Network performance issues** caused by congestion or node failures.
* **Economic imbalances** in staking or reward distribution.
* **Governance disputes** or low participation affecting DAO efficiency.
* **Force majeure events** such as geopolitical or infrastructural disruptions.

ARX’s distributed governance and phased decentralization are designed to minimize the impact of these risks through flexibility and transparency.
