# 8.4 Deflation, Sustainability, and Governance Mechanisms

ARX maintains a balanced monetary system through controlled supply, adaptive governance, and revenue-backed deflation.

### Supply and deflation controls

* **Tail emission**: Annual inflation starts at 4% and declines linearly to 0% after ten years.
* **DAO buybacks**: The DAO can repurchase tokens using surplus service revenues and remove them from circulation.
* **Fee recycling**: 10–15% of all collected network and subscription fees are automatically burned.
* **Validator slashing**: 0.5–2% of tokens from underperforming validators are burned.

### Governance integration

All key parameters, including staking rewards, emission reduction rate, and buyback ratios, are decided through DAO proposals and voted on-chain. Token holders directly control economic policy, ensuring transparency and adaptability as the network grows.

With this structure, ARX transitions from emission-funded incentives to a stable, service-driven economy that scales sustainably.
