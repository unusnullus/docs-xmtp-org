# 12.4 Validator and community partnerships

Validators and local communities play a central role in ARX’s decentralization.

* Validator programs: Reward uptime, latency, and security performance.
* Regional hubs: Local partnerships to establish relay zones and promote adoption.
* Community initiatives: Hackathons, bounties, and educational programs to expand participation.

These relationships strengthen ARX’s infrastructure diversity and align its governance with community-led growth.

Reach us via [info@arx.pro](mailto:info@arx.pro) to explore partnerships.
