# 9.3 Governance roles

Participation in ARX governance is open to all network stakeholders, structured through three functional roles: validators, delegators, and a temporary governance council.

### Validators

Operate network nodes, validate transactions, and produce blocks. They participate directly in governance by voting on proposals related to staking, upgrades, and treasury allocations. Their influence and rewards depend on performance and stake size.

### Delegators

Token holders who delegate their ARX to validators. They earn a share of staking rewards and indirectly participate in governance through their chosen validator. This allows all token holders to contribute without running infrastructure.

### Governance Council (Transitional Phase)

A temporary body composed of trusted validators, legal advisors, and ecosystem contributors. It maintains operational stability during the early stages of governance. Responsibilities include supervising upgrades, managing emergency actions, and supporting onboarding. As participation grows, the council’s authority is phased out, transferring full control to DAO voting.

This role distribution ensures both expert oversight and inclusive participation across all stages of network growth.
