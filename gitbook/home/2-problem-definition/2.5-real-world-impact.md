# 2.5 Real-World Impact

The practical effects of these weaknesses are visible across nearly every sector.

* **Data breaches**: Repeated leaks from major service providers have compromised billions of records, exposing personal messages, credentials, and financial data.
* **Censorship and access restrictions**: Messaging platforms, media outlets, and social networks have been blocked or throttled in entire countries during political events or crises.
* **Custodial failures**: Users have lost access to funds and accounts when payment processors or exchanges suspended operations due to regulatory pressure or financial instability.

These incidents reveal a consistent pattern. Users are dependent on intermediaries that control both infrastructure and policy. Trust in these systems relies not on transparency or cryptography but on the continued goodwill of the operators.

The internet, once envisioned as a decentralized and resilient network, now functions as a set of walled gardens governed by private and governmental interests. Without structural reform, privacy will remain conditional, access will remain revocable, and participation will remain unequal.
