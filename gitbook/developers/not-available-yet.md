# Not available yet

Check this list before designing your bot around group chats, payment flows, embedded apps or file-processing features. Choose a supported alternative from the [capability map](features.md).

- Bot conversations in groups and channels are not available.
- Connected bots and private topics are not available.
- Mini Apps, inline mode and games are not available.
- Login buttons and inline-switch buttons are not available.
- Payments, paid content and paid broadcasts are not available.
- External bot universal links, app links and deferred links are not available.
- Custom emoji are not available.
- Country-restricted polls are not available.
- [File Studio](official-bots.md#file-studio) video transcoding and quality selection are not available.
- [File Studio](official-bots.md#file-studio) OCR and audio transcription are not available.
- Viewing private bot statistics in NexoCreator is not available.
- A self-hosted Nexo Bot API server is not available.

Use the [API reference](api.md) for the operations you can call and the [changelog](changes.md) to follow public changes.
