# 14 Risk Factors

Participation in the ARX ecosystem involves exposure to a range of risks that may affect token value, network operations, or user experience.\
This section provides a transparent overview of the primary risk categories in accordance with MiCA and international regulatory requirements.

While the ARX team and DAO apply strict security, compliance, and risk management practices, no system—technological or economic—is entirely free from risk.\
Users and token holders are strongly advised to perform independent due diligence before engaging with the platform or acquiring ARX tokens.
